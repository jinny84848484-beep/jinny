Jinny Shorts Automation

A personal production tool operated by the owner of two YouTube commentary channels: 오늘의 오지랖 and A Not-So-Polite Korean.

The tool researches public news, prepares original commentary, creates narration and captioned videos, and uploads approved productions to the owner's channels. It is not offered as a public sign-in service.

Google authorization is used to identify the owner's selected YouTube channels, read video information and upload videos. The owner can revoke access in their Google account.
